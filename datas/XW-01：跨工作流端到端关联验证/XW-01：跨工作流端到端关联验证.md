记录：`task_reports.id`、`task_id`、`task_similarity` 主键、`submit_id`/`origin_id`、`similarity`。

```sql
SELECT r.id report_id, r.task_id, r.exec_status, r.max_similarity,
       s.id sim_id, s.submit_id, s.origin_id, s.similarity
FROM task_reports r
LEFT JOIN task_similarity s ON s.task_id=r.task_id AND s.deleted=0
WHERE r.problem_id='1741610183727411210' AND r.deleted=0
ORDER BY r.create_time DESC;
```
